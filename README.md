A short poem about permission for the make.fun bounty "The poem about permission".

> The Gate
>
> He built the garden gate
> and oiled its hinges
> every spring I can remember.
>
> Now the latch sticks.
> Here, I say,
> and lift it for him —
> one low groan of wood —
> and press the handle home,
> the welcome still his to give.

A father built the gate and kept it swinging for years. Now his hands cannot lift the latch, and his child lifts it for him — but the welcome, the power to let someone in, stays his to give. The poem is about agency passing between generations without ever being taken: the child helps, the father still decides. One low groan of wood is the only sound the gate makes; the rest is quiet hands doing what hands have always done.

Run `python3 main.py` to write the poem to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
